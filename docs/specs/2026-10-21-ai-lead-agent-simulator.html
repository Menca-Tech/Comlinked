    Why NorthPack Solutions: 86/100

    NorthPack is the best supplier match for Kusina's recyclable tray requirement. It is local, stocks two of the three sizes, fits the approved price band, and can deliver before the retailer's April deadline.

    CategoryMaxNorthPackGreenFormPacific Rim

    Why NorthPack was chosen

    • Two of three sizes are stock, freezer-rated and recyclable.
    • 2 to 3 week lead time meets the mid-March target with about a month to spare.
    • Indicative pricing for stock sizes sits inside Kusina's approved $0.18 to $0.24 band.
    • 28 km away on weekly GTA routes, no freight charge over 5,000 units.
    • Can supply the food-contact letters the retailer requires.

    Why the others ranked lower

    • GreenForm (66): minimum order of 100,000 per size, and a 2-day reply time.
    • Pacific Rim (64): importer with 8 to 10 week lead times, which risks the April deadline.
    • Both stay in Nurture as back-up suppliers.

    Risks to raise in the meeting

    • The two-compartment tray is a near match. A failed fit trial means 6 to 8 weeks of custom tooling.
    • No price yet for the two-compartment size.
    • New relationship: NorthPack may run a credit check before net 30.
