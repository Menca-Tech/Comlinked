How the Demo flows

The Demo is the Next.js app under /app, a simulation that runs in the browser with invented data. Nothing is sent or stored, and sign-in is pretend. The older single-file HTML mockup in docs/mockup/ is frozen as a backup. Links below open your local server at localhost:3001 while it runs.

Full Demo: one member’s daily loop

Start as a new company or jump two weeks in, then move freely. Every screen works. A full page reload starts the Demo over.

1 · Claim

“Your company is already here” → check the profile → tick the authorization box → pretend sign-in.

2 · Meet 3 matches

Each with a reason to meet. Connect, Message or Skip. The AI drafts; a person sends.

3 · ComDash

The home screen, in five tabs.

4 · The rest of the loop

  • Leads: five new matches a day
  • ComMail: conversations, AI drafts, “We met” and “We did business”
  • Asks: post a need, answer others
  • Wins: testimonials, Success Wall, Monthly Top Companies
  • Search, Notifications, your company profile
↻ Next morning The guide runs a simulated night: a new lead list, the morning digest, other companies viewing, messaging, claiming and posting wins. Then the loop starts again.

Rules worth knowing

Reloads

A reload restarts the full Demo, but inside the Pilot Preview it reopens the Stop you were on.

Repeatable

Every Stop and every start builds the same state each time, so nothing surprises the presenter.

Honest by design

A company that has not claimed shows basics only and never acts. Wins appear only from their day. “Example data” stays on every screen.
